About Emmanuel

Left Nigeria at 18. Earned a Ph.D. at Princeton.

Emmanuel left Nigeria for New York with one goal: the education he had always wanted. Twenty Ph.D. programs said no the first time. He kept going, and scholarships and fellowships carried him to a Ph.D. from Princeton and to Google, where he applies AI today.
