Personal keynote

Begin Right Here: Finding Your Why When the Answer Is No

At 18, Emmanuel left Nigeria for Queens, New York, with one goal: the education he had always wanted.

The road was full of setbacks. The first time he applied to Ph.D. programs, twenty of them turned him down. What kept him going was his why: to make an impact beyond himself, in Africa and around the world.

Scholarships and fellowships carried him through four degrees in eleven years without a single student loan, ending with a Ph.D. from Princeton. Today he applies AI at Google.

This talk is the story behind the credentials: the rejections, the people who opened doors and the habits that carried him through. Its message to anyone who feels behind is simple: you don’t have to catch up. You can begin right here.

Your audience will

  • Find their why and use it to push through setbacks
  • Treat rejection as a step forward, not a final answer
  • Learn how mentors, scholarships and fellowships open doors, and how to find them

Best for: universities, student organizations, graduations, scholarship programs and employee resource groups
