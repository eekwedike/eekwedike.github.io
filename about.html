His story

Twenty programs said no. He kept going.

Born and raised in Nigeria, Emmanuel moved to Queens, New York, at 18 in pursuit of a better education and the opportunity to build a promising future. It was a life-changing move. For many young Nigerians from a similar background, higher education is out of reach, let alone a full scholarship. Emmanuel is grateful to have found a different path, and for the scholarships and fellowships that made his dreams possible. Life in America inspired him to broaden his horizons and to make meaningful contributions to the world.

He earned a bachelor’s degree in mathematics from Brooklyn College and a master’s in applied mathematics from the City College of New York. The first time he applied to Ph.D. programs, twenty of them turned him down. He kept going, and Princeton welcomed him with a Presidential Fellowship. There he earned his Ph.D. in Operations Research and Financial Engineering.

Today, Emmanuel is a data scientist at Google, building GenAI-powered tools for business and marketing teams.
