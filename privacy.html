Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Emmanuel Ekwedike runs this site (emmanuelekwedike.com). I collect as little personal information as possible, use it only for the reason you shared it, and never sell it.

What I collect and why

When you… What I collect How I use it
Subscribe Name, email To send the updates you signed up for
Contact me Name, email, message To reply to you
Ask about booking Name, email, phone, company, event type, date, budget, details To answer your enquiry and plan any engagement
Browse the site Pages viewed, browser, device, approximate location (via Google Analytics cookies) In aggregate only, to improve the site

Like most websites, the host and the services that load scripts and video thumbnails also receive your IP address and basic browser details.

Services that handle your information

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree: receives and forwards all form submissions
  • Google: Analytics and YouTube thumbnails
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the site
  • jsDelivr, cdnjs and jQuery: deliver standard code libraries

I do not sell, rent or trade your information, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long I keep it

  • Subscriber details: until you unsubscribe.
  • Messages and booking enquiries: as long as needed to respond and keep reasonable records, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: per Google Analytics retention settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete: email me and I will respond within 30 days.
  • Stop emails: unsubscribe at any time by replying to any update or emailing me.
  • Limit analytics: block or delete cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

If you live in the EU, the UK, California or elsewhere with privacy laws, you may have further rights, such as to know what is held about you or to object to its use. Email me to exercise them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
